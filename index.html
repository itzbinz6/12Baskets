Our Story

More than
small chops.
Much more.

12 Baskets Foods began with a simple conviction — that small chops deserved the same care and craftsmanship as any fine dining dish. What started as a single obsession has grown into Lagos's most complete finger food and event catering experience.

Named after the miracle of the twelve baskets of abundance, we believe every order should leave you with more than you expected — in taste, in presentation, and in memory.

Today we serve Lekki, Ikeja, Ikorodu, Abuja, and beyond, with a full menu that runs from puff puff to lobster, from a Grab n Go Pack to a Diamond Celebration Box.

FAQ

Questions
answered.

Can't find what you need? Call our hotline on 0704 006 0000 — we're open 7am daily.

Do you cater for large events like weddings and corporate parties?
Yes — we cater for all sizes, from 10-person office lunches to 500-guest weddings and burial receptions. We have bulk pastry trays, platter packages, and full rice catering options. Call 0704 006 0000 to discuss your event.
How far in advance should I place a large order?
For large orders — platters, trays, catering packages — we recommend at least 24–48 hours' notice so we can prepare everything fresh and on time. For smaller packs, same-day ordering through Chowdeck or a direct call to your nearest branch works just fine.
Which branch delivers to my area?
Each branch handles its own delivery zone. Lekki/Island: 0802 959 7299 | Ikeja: 0708 367 3374 | Ikorodu: 0916 000 5735 | Abuja: 0805 541 8528. For online delivery, order through our Chowdeck link and it'll route to your nearest branch automatically.
Can I customise a platter or celebration box?
Absolutely. If you need to swap items, accommodate dietary preferences, or build something custom for your event, call or WhatsApp your nearest branch and we'll sort it out. Allow extra lead time for heavily customised orders.
